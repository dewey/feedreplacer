feedreplacer

Takes values from one podcast feed and puts them into the items of another. Go through the steps from top to bottom.

1. Add the two feeds

The feed you want to replace things in. Everything you do not replace is kept.

The feed that has the things you want to inject.

2. Dates (optional)

Skip this if both feeds have a publication date. Use it to match by date when one feed only has the date somewhere else, for example in the title like 2026-01-31-Episode name. The fields that hold a date show up below once both feeds are loaded.

How the date is written in text. Use YYYY, MM and DD, for example DD.MM.YYYY. The publication date field is read without a pattern.

Paste both feed URLs to continue.
